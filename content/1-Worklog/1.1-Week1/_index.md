### Week 1 Achievements:

- **Account setup and troubleshooting:**
    - Understand the phone verification roadblock and support workflow.
    - Continue foundational learning through videos and documentation during the account issue.
- **Cloud architecture and security:**
    - Differentiate Regions, Availability Zones and Edge Locations.
    - Explain the purposes of IAM users, groups, policies and temporary roles.
- **Compute, networking and storage:**
    - Describe how VPC, subnets, gateways and security groups connect EC2 and RDS.
    - Distinguish S3, EBS and EFS, and compare EC2, ECS, EKS and Lambda.
- **Cost management and AI tools:**
    - Recognize the billing and cost optimization concepts studied.
    - Understand how Kiro may support coding and cloud administration.
