### Week 2 Achievements:

- **Core service knowledge:**
    - Distinguish the compute, storage, networking and database service groups.
    - Understand the relationship between services, Regions and resource management.
- **Account and console setup:**
    - Complete Free Tier account setup as recorded in the original notes.
    - Navigate the AWS Management Console to inspect resources.
- **CLI and parallel management:**
    - Configure AWS CLI credentials and default Region.
    - Retrieve account information, Regions, key pairs and EC2 instances using CLI and compare with the console.
